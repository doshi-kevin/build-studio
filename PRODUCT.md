# Product

## Register

brand

## Users

- **Professors / course assistants**: time-poor educators replacing Canvas; build courses, run live sessions, grade. Evaluate tools on speed-to-first-course and whether students will actually use them.
- **Students**: mobile-heavy, allergic to legacy LMS friction. Arrive via their institution; the product must feel consumer-grade.
- **Institution admins**: the buyers. Evaluate on migration cost, security/compliance, and faculty adoption risk.
- **Landing-page visitor**: a faculty member or admin decision-maker assessing "can this replace Canvas for us?" in under a minute.

## Product Purpose

Scholera is an AI-native LMS replacing Canvas: courses, assessments, live classroom, analytics, and AI tutoring as one unified system instead of bolted-together tools. Multi-tenant (institutions). Launching across the United States in July 2026, built for 30,000+ students. NJ Plug and Play-backed. Success = institutions switch and faculty adopt in days, not months.

## Brand Personality

Calm, precise, confident. The shipped "Modern Clean" identity: light cool-neutral surfaces, one confident blue (oklch 0.56 0.19 260), Instrument Serif display voice paired with Geist. The product itself is the proof; show real UI, not abstractions. Never shouty, never gimmicky.

## Anti-references

- Generic AI-generated SaaS landing pages: eyebrow label above every section, identical icon-card grids, gradient text, div-built fake dashboard mockups, invented metrics.
- Dark-glow dev-tool clones (Linear/Vercel imitations) — wrong register for education and a saturated lane.
- Legacy ed-tech marketing (Canvas/Blackboard): clip-art, stocky campus photos, feature-checklist walls.
- Fabricated social proof of any kind (fake testimonials, fake named people, fake stats).

## Design Principles

1. **The product is the demo.** Real screenshots of the real app over decorative CSS scenery or abstract illustration.
2. **Honest positioning.** Only defensible claims; no fake testimonials, people, or numbers. Pre-launch honesty is the brand.
3. **One brand, landing to app.** Marketing surfaces use the app's shipped token system (Modern Clean), amplified — not a second identity.
4. **Motion with intent, never at the cost of speed.** Transform/opacity-only choreography, reduced-motion fallbacks, no scroll-listener jank, lazy-load below the fold.
5. **Calm confidence over noise.** Committed color moments and generous space instead of dense persuasion tactics.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

WCAG AA contrast (4.5:1 body, 3:1 large text), `prefers-reduced-motion` alternative for every animation, full keyboard paths, semantic landmarks/headings, touch targets ≥ 44px.
